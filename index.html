Every set counts.

SBL Gym is a free Android training log built on Science Based Lifting. It builds a split for your gym, logs every set to failure with a rest timer, and tells you after each session whether to add weight or reps.

A split for your gym

Pick FBEOD, ULUL, APAP, PPLUL or TLTL, or build your own. Exercises are chosen from the equipment you have and checked against SBL rules: 5–10 reps to failure, at least 2 sets per muscle per session, every muscle twice a week, 48 hours of recovery.

Progress you can act on

Each session is compared with the last one, exercise by exercise: add weight, add reps, or lighten. Weekly and monthly reports show frequency, volume, body weight and streaks.

Food and nutrients

Calorie, macro, micronutrient and water tracking from a food list, by grams per ingredient, or by scanning a barcode. Optional photo estimates and an AI coach run on a free on-device model that works offline, or on Claude or Gemini with your own API key.

AI that stays on your phone

Download a free model once and get photo calorie estimates, meal parsing and a coach that work offline. Nothing you log or photograph leaves the phone.

Body progress

Tape measurements with trends and a body-fat estimate, plus progress photos kept only on your phone with a before-and-after slider.

See your recovery

A front and back body map shows which muscles are recovered, which need another day, and where your weekly volume goes.

Your data stays yours

SBL Gym has no servers and no accounts of its own. You can use it as a guest, with everything stored only on your phone, or sign in with Google to keep your log in sync across phones.

When you sign in with Google, SBL Gym asks for:

AccessWhy
Your email address and basic profile (name, photo)To show which Google account is signed in and greet you by name. Kept on your phone only.
Its own app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata)To save your training log in your own Drive so it syncs between your phones. This is a private, hidden folder only SBL Gym can use. The app can't see, open or change any of your other Drive files.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no selling of data. The developer never receives your training log. Optional features talk to other services only when you use them: an AI provider you choose with your own key, Hugging Face once to download the on-device model, and Open Food Facts for barcode lookups. Details are in the privacy policy.

Get the app

SBL Gym is distributed as an Android app (APK) by its developer. It needs Android 7.0 or newer.
